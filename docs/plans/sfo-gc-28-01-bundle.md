**Decisions (user 2026-09-22):**
- #454/#461, a TAXI whose named route doesn't reach its destination: every named taxiway is taxied for real (never swapped for a sibling). After the last one the aircraft may continue to the destination only across ramp / non-movement taxilanes (the ramp-lane cut: T5A → alley → T5B side → D1 is fine). - #455 and #457 share one cause (aviation review 2026-09-22, bundle server log lines 450–458): `GroundCommandHandler`'s current-taxiway prepend (~L168-186) turns a re-issued `TAXI A F 28L` into `B A F` (southbound entry onto A, U-turn across 01L/19R; the first issue resolved correctly to `B B1 A F`), and `TAXI A` after holding short of K into `B A`. Fix: prepend the current taxiway only when the as-cleared route cannot start from where the aircraft stands; the taxiway an aircraft holds short of counts as where it stands.
- #456 (user 2026-09-23): a `TAXI … $spot` given to an aircraft on the ramp is a line-up to leave the ramp: it arrives on the spot from the ramp side and stops nose-on-mark facing out along the lane toward the movement-area taxiway the lane joins (SFO `TAXI T7A $7A` → facing ~027° toward A). It never swings out toward the taxiway to turn back in. The mirror case (user 2026-09-23): `TAXI A $7A @F8` from the movement area enters the ramp through that spot — off A onto T7A at the junction, through 7A heading into the ramp, along T7A's taxilane — then turns into the gate. Shape of the outbound line-up (user 2026-09-23, on seeing it render): not a sharp loop far down the lane — a smoother turn of about 90° onto the lane close to the spot, then a short slow straight pull onto the mark once lined up. Also to render and review: `PUSH $7A`, `PUSH $7` and `PUSH $7B` from the nearby gates — the RPOs' main way to get an aircraft off a gate onto a spot. Ruled out (2026-09-22, rendered at `.tmp/456-options` in the taxi-456 worktree): a lead-in point on the lane's extended line (nose 13 ft from A's centreline, ending facing 212° into the ramp) and the plain approach leg (ends facing 227°).
- Movement vs non-movement for the #454 rule: `RampLaneReposition.IsRampTaxilane` (name heuristic, also behind `TugPavementClassifier.IsMovementArea`) marks SFO's AF, AY1–AY4, B1–B5, BC, CG, CZ, Q1, Z2, ZS as non-movement.
