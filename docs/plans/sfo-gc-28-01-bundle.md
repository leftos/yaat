**Decisions (user 2026-09-22):**
- #454/#461, a TAXI whose named route doesn't reach its destination (shipped 2026-09-23): every named taxiway is taxied for real, never swapped for a sibling. From the named lane a stand is cut to directly: across the apron to a point one fuselage out on the stand's centreline, then in on the stand heading (user-approved from the D1 renders); the sibling-lane cut is the fallback. After the last named taxiway the aircraft continues only across ramp taxilanes and the apron, plus an implied lead-in (the destination's own taxiway, only apron after it, ≤ 1,000 ft driven). If reaching the destination needs any other movement-area taxiway, it taxis the named route and holds short of that taxiway (`HoldShortReason.RouteIncomplete`), with the note `Holding short of X: route to <dest> needs X, not in clearance`, and the pilot says "…request further taxi". A runway-entry connector that isn't a numbered variant always holds short; a numbered one (W → W1) is implied. The echo and pilot readback say the clearance as issued: added taxilanes and the implied connector are silent, other uncleared movement-area taxiways are warned. Reference: `docs/ground/pathfinder.md` (Incomplete clearances), `COMMANDS.md` (TAXI).
- #456 (user 2026-09-23): a `TAXI … $spot` given to an aircraft on the ramp is a line-up to leave the ramp: it arrives on the spot from the ramp side and stops nose-on-mark facing out along the lane toward the movement-area taxiway the lane joins (SFO `TAXI T7A $7A` → facing ~027° toward A).
