Six more approach flyouts: heading and glyphs

X3b gives Join approach (JAPP), Cleared straight-in (CAPPSI), Join straight-in (JAPPSI), Cleared approach (force) (CAPPF), Join approach (force) (JAPPF) and Join final approach course (JFAC) the tabbed flyout X3a built for Cleared approach and Expect approach, one flyout per verb, the verb fixed by its All Commands entry. Ruled already: none of the six has visual rows, the untowered airport row or the Force toggle, and the force pair's rows are always forced, with the force mark.

This page asks two things: whether the six flyouts get a title line naming the verb, and what glyph their rows carry. Each artboard has lanes A, B and C; no lane is marked recommended. Click a row or a tab to see what it does; the boxes take typing.

Every flyout draws the built X3a panel (ApproachFlyoutPanel): the default row pinned first with keyboard focus, a separator, the runway tabs in the room's order with each tab's row count, the selected tab's rows (glyph, name, badges, kind, command in JetBrains Mono at 70%), a separator and the Approach box. Without visual rows, 15 and 33 have no approach and get no tab. A forced row wears the force mark (the filled amber “force” badge and the amber command). The Join, straight-in and JFAC glyphs and the glyph force mark (an amber disc with “!”) are drawings for this decision, in the glyph style of QuickCommandGlyphs: a 24 px view box, stroke 1.9, round caps, the flight blue #A8CAFF. The title lines for the six are drawn as TitleLine draws Cleared approach's. Cancel IFR is left out of every flyout. The dashed notes are mock annotations.
