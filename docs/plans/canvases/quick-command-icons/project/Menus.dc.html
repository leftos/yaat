Anatomy

{{s.name}}
  • 40 px cells, 6 px apart, five to a row, at most two rows.
  • The glyph's color is its family: tower amber, ground teal, flight blue, pattern violet, scope grey.
  • The corner notch means a picker opens (runway, heading, altitude) instead of sending at once.
  • The line under the strip names the hovered or focused command and its text; it doubles as the tooltip.
  • Arrow keys move across the strip, Enter sends; Tab moves on to the text entries.
  • A quick command with no glyph drops to a text row under Command….
