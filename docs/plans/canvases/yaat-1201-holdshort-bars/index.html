KOAK hold-short bars: painted (MAP) vs generated (GND)

YAAT-1201 (GitHub #1352, "That doesn't look like its holding short..."). The Ground View's MAP layer draws the vNAS tower-cab videomap, whose hold-short bars are plain 2-point yellow lines; GND draws YAAT's generated hold-short nodes as a ±30 ft tick. At KOAK they disagree. Each row below is one intersection; each lane is one way MAP could draw it. The question: which lane should MAP show?

Measured over all of KOAK: 35 painted bars, 31 generated nodes, 31 pairs (same runway and side, within 200 ft); 4 painted bars and 0 generated nodes unpaired. Paired painted bars sit 3-70 ft farther from the runway centerline than YAAT's nodes on 28L, 28R and 30/12 (median +11, +28 and +32 ft); on 15/33 they straddle it (−18 to +18 ft). Seven pairs are over 50 ft apart; four differ in angle by more than 10° (V01 24°, V09 36°, V10 45°, V35 50°).

On the satellite image, at all three intersections below, the painted bar lies on the real marking (within 4 ft and 5°) and YAAT's bar does not (30-128 ft off). The client's tick angle matches a true perpendicular to the taxiway at every OAK node (within 3°), so the angle gap at E comes from the paint running parallel to the runway, not from the tick's angle rule.
