YAAT-101: nav-route overlay, latent restrictions and route-fix holds

Scene: AAL123 on a STAR at FL245, cleared "descend and maintain one one thousand" (not descend via), so every published altitude on the STAR is latent. ATC also said "cross MIDDY at 7000" (a CFIX), which is active. A hold at MIDDY (right turns, inbound on the arrival course, 1-minute legs) is queued for when the aircraft reaches the fix.

Drawn from the real renderer: route colour is palette slot 0 #FF6B6B, 2 px non-antialiased lines, 5 px diamonds, fix name in 12 px monospace at (+8, -4), restriction lines in 10 px monospace from (+8, +8) stepping 11 px, dashed leader 1 px at alpha 150, dashed shapes [10, 5]. Scale 12 px/nm; racetrack radius and leg are to scale for 230 KIAS at 7000. Published speeds (250, 210) are drawn active in every lane: a maintain clearance leaves published speeds in force, and whether a speed can ever be latent is a separate open decision. The aircraft symbol and datablock are schematic.

Question 1: how does a latent restriction label look?

Only the restriction labels change between lanes. The queued hold is not drawn here (today's code draws no upcoming hold; Question 2 covers it).

Question 2: how does an upcoming hold (at a fix ahead, not yet holding) look?

Labels drawn as today in every lane, so only the racetrack changes. Today an active hold (HoldingPatternPhase) draws solid; the racetrack's inbound leg lies on the route's last leg, as a hold on the arrival course does.

Question 3: VFR and airspace-boundary orbits (no inbound course or legs)

Scale 30 px/nm (a terminal-area zoom). N123AB (slot 0) was told to orbit at visual point VPBRG (HFIX, VfrHoldPhase) and is still proceeding to it. N456CD (slot 1, #4ECDC4) is orbiting outside the Class C boundary (grey) awaiting entry (AirspaceBoundaryHoldPhase, Orbit mode). Circle radius is the standard-rate radius at each aircraft's speed (100 and 120 KTAS, about 0.53 and 0.64 nm). The fix name keeps its usual (+8, -4) spot, so at this zoom the circle runs through its first letter; the real renderer would do the same unless the label moves.
