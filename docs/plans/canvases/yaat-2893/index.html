YAAT-2893 · Hiding the Radar’s bar to a small SHOW tile: open look questions

The owner asked to hide the DCB to a tiny SHOW DCB tile, extended to all three bars: the STARS DCB, the ERAM Master Toolbar and the classic Mixed (navy) bar. Two questions remain; pick one option per question. Each lane shows, for each bar, a 1280 px Radar window with the bar shown and then hidden, at the sizes and colours the code uses. Callsigns and values are examples. Pink dashed notes and the dashed cyan line (the scope’s top edge while the bar shows) are mock annotations, not UI.

  1. Q1 Where the SHOW tile sits once the bar is hidden: A top-left corner of the scope · B top-right corner of the scope
  2. Q2 How the user hides the bar: A a HIDE tile on each bar · B right-click the bar’s empty area, or .scope bar hide
Q1 · ASHOW tile in the scope’s top-left corner

Hiding the bar collapses its row, so the scope grows up into the freed rows and the SHOW tile hangs over its top-left corner, where the bar began.
Each SHOW tile is drawn in its bar’s own style at that bar’s smallest tile size: a DCB Half tile, an ERAM button, a navy half tile.

Q2 · BNo tile: right-click the bar, or type the command

The bars keep their tiles as they are. A right-click on a bar’s empty area opens a one-item menu, Hide bar, and .scope bar hide does the same.
The SHOW tile, or .scope bar show, brings the bar back. The menu is drawn open; the SHOW tile is drawn at Q1 A’s corner.
