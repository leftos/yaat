Land for an airborne helicopter: how NODEL is offered

An airborne helicopter gets a Land flyout with the Air taxi flyout's look: a filter box on top (Enter sends the top match), then two tabs, Pads · parking and Spots, with live distances; no Runways tab and no chips. Spot rows send LAND $NAME, pads and parking LAND @NAME. LAND also takes a NODEL suffix (LAND @HELI2 NODEL), which exempts the aircraft from auto-delete after it lands. The one open choice here is how the flyout offers NODEL; each lane draws the same flyout with one answer, and no lane is marked as recommended.

Data is real: N812HL, an EC35, airborne 1.2 nm south of KOAK's north field (a point 1.2 nm due south of HELI1, over the south field), with KOAK's three helicopter positions (HELI, HELI1, HELI2, all parking features), 165 other parking positions and 16 spots from the bundled oak.geojson. Distances are straight-line from that point, rounded to 50 ft, measured for this mock; in the app they follow the aircraft as it flies. Click rows, chips, tabs and check boxes, or type in a filter box, to see what each sends.

Row templates, tabs, the menu surface and the strip follow air-taxi-mock.html beside this page: Inter text, commands in JetBrains Mono at 70%, 22 px glyphs at stroke 1.9 in the ground colour, the 480 px cap of MenuCommandRow.InlineRowMaxWidth, tabs as FlyoutTabStrip draws them. The Land glyph (rotor, body, skids, down arrow) is a placeholder drawn for this mock, as the Air taxi glyph is. The check box is drawn after Avalonia Fluent's dark check box. The amber NODEL in a row's command is a mock highlight; the app prints the command in one colour. The page loads no web fonts, so it draws in Segoe UI and Consolas where Inter and JetBrains Mono are not installed.
