YAAT-569 · part X4 · the ground point menu's strip · mock for the owner's decisions

Point menu flyouts: Taxi here, Taxi to runway, Push to

The owner's rule for YAAT-569 is that a quick command never opens a nested context submenu. Two places still build one, both in the ground point menu (the right-click on a taxi node, a runway or a stand with an aircraft selected): Taxi here with several routes (MenuCatalog.BuildPointTaxiHere) and Taxi to {end} (BuildTaxiToRunwayEnds), up to four menus deep. Push to is a single command with no choice. This page draws each open decision as lanes A, B (and C); no lane is marked recommended.

Every flyout draws the landed parts as built: the Fluent dark menu, the tab strip of FlyoutTabStrip, the title lines and two-line rows (MenuTwoLineRow) of Taxi to runway, the glyph rows of MenuGlyphRow, the 480 px cap. The aircraft is UAL455, A320, at KOAK gate 25; taxiway, runway, spot and node names are from the bundled layout (tests/Yaat.Sim.Tests/TestData/oak.geojson). Click a row, a tab or a strip button to see what it does.

Index note: settled by recommendation, not drawn as a choice

  • 5 · Route preview. recommended: as Taxi to runway (X2b): the row under the pointer, else the keyboard-focused row, previews its route on the ground scope through SetRoutePreview (dashed); a title line or a tab draws nothing and leaves the last preview; choosing another tab clears it, and closing the flyout clears it. Today's point submenus never clear it on pointer leave (MenuCatalog.BuildChoiceSubmenu).
  • 6 · Custom taxi…. recommended: keep it its own strip button opening the host's input popup, seeded as today (GetCustomTaxiSeed), lane A below. A box inside the Taxi here flyout (lane B) has nowhere to live at a node with no route, where Custom taxi is needed most. Drawn as two small lanes.
  • 7 · Keyboard. recommended: the X2b model: focus lands on the first row on open, so Enter sends it; Up and Down (and Tab, Shift+Tab) walk the rows, skipping titles and stopping at the ends; Left and Right choose the previous or next tab where there are tabs, focusing its first row; tabs never take focus; Escape closes the flyout back to its strip button. The point menu has no All Commands, so its text rows (Taxi here ›, Taxi to 30 ›) open the same builder's flyout.
  • 9 · Screen edge and width. Already ruled (X2b ruling 5): FlipX | SlideY, no list shrinking, the 480 px cap, long two-line rows wrap and are never trimmed.
Rows follow the built flyouts: Inter text, commands in JetBrains Mono at 70% (one-line rows, trimmed at 180 px as MenuCommandRow.RouteCommandMaxWidth) or whole under the label (two-line rows), 22 px glyphs at stroke 1.9 in the ground colour #6EE0CE, 40 px strip cells with the corner notch when a button opens a flyout, Push back to's badges in PushbackToMenu.BadgeBorder's colours. The strip glyphs are the built point glyphs (QuickCommandGlyphs.PointById): Taxi here the pin, Taxi to runway the runway arrow, Push to the push-back arrow, Custom taxi the pencil. Route distances are measured along the layout's centrelines for this mock, not by the client's pathfinder. The scope insets are separate SVG files in img/. The dashed notes, the pointer and the crosshair on the insets are mock annotations. The page loads no web fonts.
