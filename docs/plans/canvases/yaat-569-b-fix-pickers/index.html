YAAT-569 · part B · the fix pickers · mock for the owner's decisions

Fix picker flyouts: direct to, hold, cross, depart, airways, radials, PTAC

The strip's Direct to…, Hold at fix… and Cross fix buttons, and their All Commands siblings (Append direct to, Hold at fix left / right, Depart fix, Join airway, Join radial outbound / inbound, PTAC), still open popups drawn by MenuPopups: a 200 px type-to-filter list over every fix with the route fixes listed until you type, a 120 px list of route fixes, a leaf beside an “(other)” input popup, a filter list followed by a bearing popup, and one-box input popups for the holding pattern and PTAC. This page draws each decision as lanes A, B (and C), the recommended lane first.

Every flyout draws the built parts: the Fluent dark menu, the title line and glyph rows, the boxes with their command column, the FlyoutTabStrip tabs, the toggle and chips of the heading flyout, the 480 px cap, and, for the radial, the heading flyout's HeadingRose. The aircraft is UAL455, an A320 from KGEG to KOAK on GEG J3 IMB J3 LKV J3 RBL SPAMY WNDSR2, at FL240, 280 kt, heading 184, 31 NM past Red Bluff (RBL) and navigating to SPAMY. Click a row, a chip, a toggle or a tab, and type in the boxes, to see what each sends.

Settled by the owner before the mock (drawn as given, not as lanes)

  • Every quick command gets a designed flyout. No fix picker stays a popup or a nested context submenu.
  • Single-box inputs become inline boxes. The “(other)” fix and airway inputs, the radial bearing, the holding pattern and PTAC become boxes inside these flyouts.
  • All Commands opens the same flyout, from one builder. Each All Commands entry opens its flyout preset to its verb.
  • Flyouts live-refill (X5 decision 6, lane A): every second in place; rows never move. Here the distance and bearing columns, the ● next-fix mark and the aircraft's radial follow the aircraft; a fix the aircraft passes greys to “passed” and keeps its place until the flyout opens again. Decision 1 lane A plays it at 10×.

Recommended, not drawn as lanes

  • Keyboard. recommended: focus lands in the fix box on open (as today's filter popup and Follow's filter box do). Typing filters; the first match is outlined and Enter sends it, else the typed name upper-cased, as today. Down moves into the rows from the box, Up from the first row returns to it; Tab walks the extra boxes and chips; Escape closes the flyout back to its strip button.
  • FRD in the box. recommended: the fix box reads a fix-radial-distance (RBL184031) as DCT does, naming the point under the box before Enter sends it.
  • Glyphs. recommended: reuse Direct to's, Hold's and Cross fix's strip glyphs, the point menu's Hold left / right racetracks for the 360s at a fix, Cleared approach's for PTAC, and the heading, altitude and speed glyphs on the boxes. Append direct to, Depart fix, Join airway and Join radial have no glyph; the page draws drafts.
  • Screen edge and width. Already ruled (X2b ruling 5): FlipX | SlideY, no shrinking of lists, the 480 px cap.

Found in the code while drawing this (static reads, not run)

  • Cross fix and Depart fix send half a command. Their route-fix list and leaf send CFIX SPAMY and DEPART SPAMY (BuildOneManyOrInput; MenuCatalogCommandTests asserts CFIX SUNOL), while ApproachCommandParser.ParseCfix refuses a fix alone (“CFIX requires fix name and altitude”) and ParseDepart wants exactly a fix and a heading. Decision 6 gives both their second value.
  • Join radial sends a spaced bearing. The bearing popup sends JRADO RBL 184; ParseRadialCommand reads one token whose last three characters are the radial (RBL184), so the fix it looks up is “RBL ” with a space. The popup also passes a typed 90 unpadded. COMMANDS.md documents the spaced form (JRADO SJC 150). The flyouts on this page send the parser's form, JRADO RBL184.
  • No distances to draw yet. IMenuAircraft carries no position, so a distance and bearing column needs one added (client side; the fix positions come from NavigationDatabase.GetFixPosition). “Nearby” lists need a scan of GetFixTuples, and airways through a fix need a fix-to-airway index over GetAirwayFixes; neither query exists.
The aircraft menu follows the built quick-command menu (bold title, state line, Command…, the strip of 40 px cells with the corner notch on a button that opens a flyout). Glyphs are the built QuickCommandGlyphs paths at 22 px (rows) and 32 px (strip), stroke 1.9, in the flight blue #A8CAFF; the drafts for Append direct to, Depart fix, Join airway and Join radial are the page's. Rows copy the rich list's marks: ● and the filled #2F4A6E row with a 3 px #5B8DEF edge on the next fix, passed fixes greyed #7D838B, a picked fix outlined in the flight blue. Fix positions, airways and names come from the repo's FAACIFP18.gz; the page embeds the 1,054 fixes within 150 NM of the aircraft, where the app searches every fix. Bearings are magnetic with a fixed 13° east variation. The dashed notes and the demo controls are mock annotations. The page loads no web fonts and has no image files.
