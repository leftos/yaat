Approach flyout: six open choices

Every lane draws the ruled first-batch lane B: one flyout for the strip, All Commands and the “(other)” row, with a runway tab bar (the runway and its approach count), the chosen runway's approaches as glyph rows (procedure name, kind, command), a separator, and the “Approach” box sending CAPP <id> or EAPP <id>. Tabs and rows use the colours of the built FlyoutTabStrip and ExitFlyout. Each artboard asks one open question, with lanes A, B and C; no lane is marked recommended.

Approach ids, procedure names and runways come from the bundled CIFP (tests/Yaat.Sim.Tests/TestData/FAACIFP18.gz), named as ApproachPickerBuilder names them. Callsigns, aircraft states and the room's runway configuration are illustrative. Click a row, a tab or a segment to see what it does; the boxes take typing.

Rows follow the first-batch lane B (quick-command-flyouts.html, section 8): Inter text, the kind in 11 px grey, commands in JetBrains Mono at 70%, 22 px glyphs at stroke 1.9 in the flight colour, the Fluent dark menu surface, strip cells 40 px with the corner notch when the button opens a flyout. Join, straight-in, force and JFAC have no glyph (QuickCommandGlyphs), so their rows show an empty dashed glyph slot. Amber outlined badges are the picker's existing “expected” badge style; the filled amber “force” badge and amber force commands are a drawing of what “visibly distinct” could be, not built UI. The pointer, the dashed notes and the “flyout” / “nested” pills are mock annotations. The page loads no web fonts.
