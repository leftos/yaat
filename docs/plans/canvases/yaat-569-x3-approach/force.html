Force approach and greyed visual rows

The owner ruled that a visual row without FOLLOW is live once the pilot has reported the field in sight, a FOLLOW row once the traffic is reported, and that a “Force approach” checkbox in the Cleared approach flyout overrides both; every approach row on a runway short for the type is dimmed “short” but still clickable. This page asks where the checkbox sits, how a greyed visual row looks, and what ticking it does. Each artboard has lanes A and B (and C for the first); no lane is marked recommended.

Every flyout draws the ruled design (index.html and visuals.html beside this page): tabs in the room's runway order, a Visual RWY n row at the top of each runway tab, glyph rows, the Approach box. The checkbox is the only new control. Approaches, runways and their lengths are real (bundled CIFP and NavData, aircraft profiles); the callsign, the aircraft state and the room's runway setup are illustrative. Click a row, a tab or a checkbox to see what it does.

Rows follow the built MenuGlyphRow as visuals.html draws them: Inter text, a 12 px note after “ · ”, commands in JetBrains Mono at 70%, the whole row at 50% when dimmed, 22 px glyphs at stroke 1.9 in the flight colour; the visual rows carry the Cleared visual eye. A row that sends a forced command wears the mark of the Cleared approach (force) flyout (index.html, decision 1 lane B): the filled amber “force” badge and the amber command. The checkbox is drawn as a Fluent dark CheckBox; the tooltip as a Fluent tooltip. FOLLOW rows are not drawn: no flyout mock has one yet. The pointer and the dashed notes are mock annotations. The page loads no web fonts and shows no image files.
