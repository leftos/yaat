Visual approaches and Cancel IFR

The owner ruled that the Cleared approach and Expect approach flyouts offer visual approaches, that an untowered field gets one airport-level CVA {airport} row, and that Cancel IFR (CIFR) is offered behind a confirmation. Which runways get a row (RunwayLandability.IsLandable, a short runway dimmed, not hidden), where Cancel IFR sits and its wording are an orchestrator decision open to the owner. This page asks how they look. Each artboard has lanes A, B and C; no lane is marked recommended.

Every flyout draws the ruled design (index.html beside this page): tabs in the room's runway order, the first selected, wrapping inside 480 px; glyph rows; the Approach box. Approaches, runways and their lengths are real (bundled CIFP and NavData, aircraft profiles); callsigns, aircraft states, the room's runway setup and the weather are illustrative. Click a row, a tab or a button to see what it does.

Rows follow the built MenuGlyphRow: Inter text, a 12 px note after “ · ”, commands in JetBrains Mono at 70%, the whole row at 50% when dimmed, 22 px glyphs at stroke 1.9 in the flight colour. The visual rows use the existing Cleared visual glyph (the eye, QuickCommandGlyphs); the dashed eye on Expect visual rows, the “Visual” tab and section titles, the untowered note, the confirmation strip, the dialog and the armed row are drawings of the options, not built UI. Cancel IFR has no glyph, so its row shows an empty dashed glyph slot. EAPP VIS28R stands in for the expect-visual token the sim sub-issue will define. The pointer and the dashed notes are mock annotations. The page loads no web fonts.
