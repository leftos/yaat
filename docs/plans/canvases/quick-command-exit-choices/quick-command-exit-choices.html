Exit flyouts: four open choices

The settled layouts are drawn in every lane: Exit left on the rollout as first-batch lane A (title, glyph rows with taxiway, “planned” note, distance and EL <twy>, a separator, Pilot's choice EL), and Exit right on final as first-batch lane B (Pilot's choice ER first, exits with “from thr” on every distance, then the “Exit at” box). Each artboard below asks one open question with two options, A and B; under each option, how it works and its worst case in use.

Exits, sides and turn-off angles are KOAK 28R and KSFO 28L from the bundled layouts (LayoutInspector --exits); distances are measured along the centreline from the node positions and rounded to 100 ft as the server rounds them. Click a row to see what it sends; the boxes take typing.

Row templates follow the first-batch mock (docs/plans/canvases/quick-command-flyouts/), the live-refill mock (docs/plans/canvases/quick-command-live-refill/) and the takeoff flyout (docs/plans/canvases/takeoff-flyout/): Inter text, commands in JetBrains Mono at 70%, 22 px glyphs at stroke 1.9 in the tower colour, the Fluent dark menu surface, an empty box showing ··· in place of its value and sending nothing. The pointer, the tooltip and the dashed notes are mock annotations, not app UI.
