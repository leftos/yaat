YAAT-2490 · Scenario Editor: the General tab

The General tab fills the Scenario Editor's content area once a scenario is opened from the sidebar (the approved YAAT-2489 window: toolbar on top, Local | Server tabs on the left, status bar at the bottom). Every lane shows the same ZOA scenario, the local copy SFO_TWR - SFO Tower Rush: student SFO_TWR (LC SFOW), minimum rating Student2, primary airport SFO with approach I28L, auto-delete Parked, four ATC positions from ZOA's SFO and NCT configs, two initialization triggers, one arrival generator and one strip bay. Positions, frequencies and bays are ZOA's real ones. Times show as ScenarioTime formats them (m:ss under an hour, h:mm:ss from an hour). Each question is independent: where a lane needs a layout it borrows the G1 Lane A page and the G2 Lane A rows, which presumes neither answer. Drawn at real pixel size in YAAT's dark theme; a dashed edge marks where a crop cuts the window.

G3 · An invalid time

The author types a time the parser rejects. How should the tab say so?

The example is 1.75, not 1:75: ScenarioTime.Parse accepts overflowing groups, so 1:75 is a valid 135 seconds and would show as 2:15 once the field is left. A decimal, a letter, a sign or an empty colon group is what the parser rejects.
