Report traffic in sight and Follow traffic flyouts

Today Report traffic in sight… opens a rich-row popup of the five nearest airborne aircraft (with no traffic, a bare text box), and Follow traffic… opens a bare text box, “Traffic callsign (optional)” (MenuCatalog.cs :171, :409–415, :1360–1423). The owner's ruling makes them one traffic-picker family.

The page draws the family three ways (lanes A, B, C), each over an excerpt of the radar scope: the flyout as it opens with the pointer on a row (its aircraft ringed on the scope), then the filter matching nothing, the empty state, and the lane's second verb. Rows, boxes and toggles are live: point at a row to ring its aircraft, type in a box, click a row or press Enter; the line under each lane says what would be sent.

The situation: N52TW, a PA28 VFR inbound to KOAK from the southeast at 2,000 ft, heading 300, for a left pattern to 28L. It has already reported N738SP (left downwind 28L) in sight. Seven other aircraft are airborne within 7 NM. Positions are drawn to the clock and distance each row states; they are illustrative, not from a recording.

Carried from earlier parts (drawn as given, not as lanes)

  • Flyout language. Title line, a glyph box row whose command column previews what Enter sends, a separator, a 300 px list, rows that show the command they send (parts B and C).
  • Filter box and keys (ground Follow, ruling 3C/5A): focus opens in the box; a case-insensitive callsign substring (here also the type) filters, nearest first kept; Enter sends the top row, else the verb with what was typed; with no match every row hides and the hint alone says so. Down into the rows, Up from the first back to the box, Escape closes.
  • Greyed row (approach flyout, “field not in sight”): 50%, not clickable, skipped by the keyboard, a note saying why, the sim's refusal in the tooltip. Force (approach and PTAC): a small toggle in the title line turning rows forced, commands in amber; absent in solo training.
  • Empty (ground ruling 2C): with no other airborne aircraft the strip button stays, faded and inert, its label line naming why; a list that empties while open shows one muted line.
  • Scope feedback on hover and keyboard focus alike, cleared when the row loses both and when the flyout closes. Here it rings the aircraft on the radar, which the ground family's ruling did not cover.

Found while drawing (static reads, not run)

  • Solo training refuses every RTIS the menu sends today. RTIS {callsign} and bare RTIS are RPO conveniences; solo needs RTIS <clock> <miles> <direction> <type> [altitude] (NavigationCommandHandler.cs:1530). A row knows all five, so it can send the descriptive form: switch Solo training above to see it.
  • “Any traffic (no target)” is refused unless traffic is already in sight (“Unable, no traffic specified”, NavigationCommandHandler.cs:1557). The lanes drop it.
  • FOLLOW checks only that some traffic is in sight, not that it is the traffic named (CommandDispatcher.cs:4090–4103): after reporting N738SP, FOLLOW SWA2287 is accepted today. The lanes grey every row but the reported one; aviation review for the sim.
  • Follow traffic… is offered after the in-sight flag has cleared: its gate reads LastReportedTrafficCallsign, which outlives the flag (MenuCatalog.cs:445, VisualApproachHelper.cs:64); it also has no All Commands entry.
  • The radar has no menu highlight. ClientMenuHost.HighlightAircraft writes ground views only, and RadarCanvas does not repaint on HighlightsChanged; the ring drawn here needs building. The client cannot show a pending “looking” RTIS (not on the wire), nor a super lead.
RTIS rows send the descriptive form; Force is gone. a dashed line from N52TW to the pointed row's aircraft, besides the ring.
