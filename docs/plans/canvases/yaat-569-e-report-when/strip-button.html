Report when… strip button

One Report when… strip button replaces At N-mile final… and At fix… in the default quick-command lists (owner ruling 8). Three things about that button are open: its glyph, whether it shows an armed mark, and which row has focus when it opens its flyout. Each is drawn below as three lanes side by side, with a line under each lane on how it works and its worst case in use.

Strips are drawn at the size the client draws them (a 40 px cell in a 1 px border, a 32 px glyph, stroke 1.9) and 3× enlarged. The flyouts follow the settled row order (E-e): the Stop section, only with something armed; the title line; the turn rows, only in a pattern circuit; Mile final, only with an assigned runway; Passing a fix; reporting points nearest first. Nothing on the page is live.

Settled already, drawn as given

  • The old two entries stay as aliases (ruling 9): At N-mile final… and At fix… keep their ids and glyphs and open the same flyout on the miles box and the fix box. A saved list may hold an alias beside the new button.
  • The button opens a submenu, so it carries the corner notch at its lower right, where the force mark also sits. Any armed mark in question 2 goes in the upper right.
  • Armed rows are marked (E-f) and the Stop section lists only what is armed (ruling 6), each armed report then Stop all reports.

The decisions, to answer as "1a, 2b, 3b"

  1. Glyph of the Report when… button: a the N-mile final glyph (runway and radio arcs), b the At fix glyph (triangle and radio arcs), c a new glyph (radio arcs over a speech mark).
  2. Armed mark on the button: a none, b a small filled dot in the upper right while any report is armed, c a count badge with the number armed.
  3. Focus when opened from the strip button: a the fix box, always, b the miles box when a runway is assigned, else the fix box, c no box (the keyboard starts on the flyout's first row).
Glyph paths are copied from QuickCommandGlyphs.cs (ApproachReportNMileFinal, ApproachReportAtFix, the pattern entries, TowerClearedToLand, PatternFollow), drawn as QuickCommandStrip.GlyphCell draws them: 32 px on the 24 px view box, stroke 1.9, round caps and joins, in the family colours (tower amber #FFC062, pattern violet #DDBDFF), the notch M6 0V6H0z in #9AA3AD on buttons that open a flyout (the pattern entries, Report when…, Follow traffic…; Cleared to land is a plain leaf). Glyph 1c, the armed dot and the count badge are drawings of options, not built UI; the dot and badge are teal #6EE0CE, the colour of the flyout's "armed" mark, ringed in the well colour as the force mark is. The neighbours are the VFR arrival default list (QuickCommandDefaults.cs, VfrArrivalInbound) in list order, with Report when… where At N-mile final… stood. Aircraft, positions and reporting-point distances and bearings are illustrative; the reporting points are the bundled ZOA visual points.
