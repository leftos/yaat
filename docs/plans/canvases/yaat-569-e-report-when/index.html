Report when… flyout

Family E is the Report when… group of All Commands › Approach: the four turn reports (REPORT CROSSWIND, DOWNWIND, BASE, FINAL), At N-mile final… (REPORT {n} FINAL), At fix… (REPORT {fix}) and the Stop reporting submenu (REPORT OFF {leg}, REPORT OFF). The two boxes are input popups today and strip defaults for VFR arrivals and departures; they send what is typed, unchecked.

The page draws three lanes for two aircraft: A rows with preset chips and checked boxes, Stop reporting as a section at the bottom; B a pattern sketch and a final ladder, Stop reporting on a tab; C one box shaped like the parser, its rows as suggestions, Stop reporting as a verb toggle. Rows, chips, tabs, toggles and boxes are live; the line under each lane says what a click or Enter sends.

Commands, refusals and the parser's rules are read from the source. The aircraft, their positions and the reporting-point distances and bearings are illustrative (approximate coordinates, 13° east declination); the reporting points and their names are the bundled ZOA visual points.

Carried from rulings and earlier parts (drawn as given, not as lanes)

  • Siblings are absorbed (owner ruling, YAAT-1018). The Report when… flyout carries the whole group, the turn reports and Stop reporting included, so no Report when… or Stop reporting submenu is left in All Commands › Approach.
  • Flyout language (parts B, C, D, F). A title line with the aircraft on the right; rows whose command column shows what a click or Enter sends; the outlined row or box is what Enter sends; a box shows its whole command beside it and a red line under it when it can send nothing.
  • A strip button opens on its own place (the heading flyout's presets): Turn left opens the shared heading flyout on its box. Here the strip's At N-mile final… and At fix… open the one Report when… flyout, focused on their own row (decision 8).
  • Nothing to pick (family F, decision 3). Where the sim has nothing to offer, the entry is disabled with the reason; the command bar covers the rest.

Found while drawing (static reads, not run)

  • The At fix box sends any REPORT form. It formats REPORT {input} (MenuCatalog.cs:181), so OFF typed as a fix name sends REPORT OFF and cancels every armed report, BASE arms the base report, and 5 FINAL arms a mile-final report (CommandParser.cs:2407-2453).
  • The N-mile box does the same. REPORT {input} FINAL (MenuCatalog.cs:180): OFF sends REPORT OFF FINAL, which cancels the turning-final report, not a mile-final one; 5.5 is refused (“unrecognized REPORT form”) after the popup has closed.
  • No single cancel for a mile-final or fix report. REPORT OFF FINAL clears only the turning-final flag (NavigationCommandHandler.cs:2290-2308); a pending REPORT 5 FINAL or REPORT VPCOL goes only with bare REPORT OFF, which also clears every turn report (AircraftApproachState.cs:142-152).
  • A cancel always answers that it cancelled. REPORT OFF BASE replies “Cancelled base report” whether or not one was armed (NavigationCommandHandler.cs:2257-2260), and no armed report reaches the client (IMenuAircraft has no such member), so the menu cannot show what is pending.
  • The parser takes report fixes of 2-5 characters only. LooksLikeFixToken (CommandParser.cs:2471-2472) refuses OAK30NUM (“Oakland Runway 30 Numbers”, ZOA/CustomFixes/oak-landmarks.json), a landmark DCT flies to.
  • The menu offers what the sim refuses. The turn reports are Always (MenuCatalog.cs:176-179) but refused outside a pattern circuit (“Unable, … is not in the pattern”, NavigationCommandHandler.cs:2228-2231); At N-mile final… is a VFR-inbound strip default (QuickCommandDefaults.cs:183) but refused with no runway assigned (“Unable, no runway assigned for final reporting”, :2236-2239), which an inbound VFR aircraft has until its pattern entry.
  • A mile-final report measures a straight line. It fires when the distance to the assigned threshold is at most N, checked only once the aircraft is inbound to land (a landing clearance, an approach, or a final-approach phase; PilotProactive.cs:198-206, ApproachCommandHandler.cs:2605-2614). One armed on a downwind 1.4 NM from the threshold fires the moment that holds.
  • A fix report never fires off the path. It fires within 0.5 NM of the fix (PilotProactive.cs:178, :220-223); a fix behind the aircraft stays armed until REPORT OFF or a full-stop landing, and a second REPORT {fix} silently replaces the first.
