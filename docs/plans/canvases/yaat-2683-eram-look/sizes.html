YAAT-2683 round 2: datablock font sizes, and a "realistic" STARS font

Round 1 settled the ERAM look (A2: ERAM glyphs, track symbols, glyph history, CRC leader, ERAM yellow) and VT323 as the ERAM face. This page answers the follow-ups: how big ERAM text should be next to STARS text, which font a "realistic" STARS setting should use, and how a Mixed scene looks with a "compact" (today) versus "realistic" font setting.

Every scope is black and drawn 1 screen pixel = 1 CRC pixel. Text sits on CRC's column pitch for the size (one glyph per cell, as CRC's monospace bitmap fonts do), and rows follow CRC's spacing rule applied to each face's own measured cap height: ERAM rows are cap + 7 px apart, STARS rows cap + 1 + CRC's line spacing for the size. "Aliased" text is drawn and then cut to full or no coverage (no grey edge pixels), the way CRC samples its bitmap fonts. Captions under each scope give the exact sizes measured in this browser.

View at 100 % browser zoom on a 100 % display scale; a scaled page blurs the pixel faces and makes everything look bigger than it would on the Scope.

Section A: ERAM size ladder

The round-1 A2 UAL123 full datablock (correlated beacon glyph over the flat-track diamond, 6 px gap then a 60 px leader NE, FDB yellow 196,196,0) and the N512RK limited block (VFR glyph, LDB yellow 129,129,0), at CRC's ERAM sizes 0 to 4. Column (a) scales VT323 so its advance equals CRC's, antialiased. Column (b) uses VT323 only where it is crisp. Column (c) uses Terminus TTF Bold, the free face with the most native bitmap sizes, at the strike whose cap height is nearest CRC's (ties go to the nearer glyph width). Terminus is drawn pixel for pixel from the bitmap strikes inside the TTF: a renderer that ignores them (as this browser does) draws the outline, which is traced from the 32 px strike and only approximates the smaller sizes.

Section B: STARS realistic vs compact

A STARS full datablock (SWA1832, yours, white) and a limited block (DAL412, another position's, green: squawk and altitude, as YAAT's stars template prints it), drawn as the Scope draws STARS tracks today (blue circle, fading blue history dots, leader to the block's box). Columns are CRC's STARS sizes: the default (2) and the next two up. Bitmap faces use their nearest native size by cap height (Terminus drawn from its strikes, Spleen 8x16 aliased); outline faces are scaled so their cap height equals CRC's and are antialiased, as YAAT draws text today.
