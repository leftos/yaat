Runway and STAR picker flyouts

Family F is every menu entry that still ends in the list popup or a bare text box because it picks a runway or a STAR: Cleared visual approach (CVA {rwy}) and its “(other)…” list, Join STAR (JARR {star}) and its “(other)…” list, the no-data text boxes of those two and of the five pattern entries, and the Custom… row of Cleared for takeoff. Converting them retires ShowListPopup.

The page draws three lanes side by side for each state: A one flyout per verb with the default pinned first, B one list with the default highlighted where it falls, C Cleared visual folded into the built Cleared approach flyout and Join STAR into a tabbed STAR flyout. Then the no-data state (two options) and the takeoff Custom row (one treatment and a variant). Rows, tabs, boxes and toggles are live; the line under each lane says what a click or Enter sends.

Runways, approaches, STARs and their transitions are real (the bundled CIFP and NavData); callsigns, positions and the room's runway setup (KOAK landing 30 and 28R) are illustrative.

Carried from earlier parts (drawn as given, not as lanes)

  • Flyout language. Title line with the aircraft on the right; glyph rows whose command column shows what a click or Enter sends; the outlined row is what Enter sends; separators; a 300 px list (parts B, C, D).
  • Approach flyout (part X3, as built): runway tabs in the room's order then the pattern-entry order, a Visual row first on each tab, the default pinned above the tabs, the airport-level CVA {airport} row at an untowered field, a visual row greyed “field not in sight” until RFIS, the Force toggle turning every row forced, Cancel IFR… last.
  • Pattern-entry runway flyout (YAAT-521): room arrival ends first, a separator, the rest, ends too short for the type dimmed “short” and still clickable.
  • Empty state (parts D, ground): an entry with nothing to offer keeps its strip button, faded and inert, its label line naming why, and its All Commands row disabled with the same note.

Found while drawing (static reads, not run)

  • Today's list cannot send its first entry. ShowList pre-selects items[0] (MenuCatalog.cs:1670, :1713; MenuPopups.cs:194-198) and sends only on SelectionChanged, so clicking the highlighted 10L or AANET1 changes nothing and sends nothing. MenuPopupsTests.cs:333 picks index 2 only.
  • Cleared visual is offered to VFR aircraft. It is a Picker with Always and MenuFlightRules.Both (MenuCatalog.cs:169, :915-916), shown in All Commands › Approach for any airborne aircraft; the sim clears only IFR aircraft for CVA (COMMANDS.md :490), which the approach flyout already gates (ApproachFlyoutModel.cs:272-276).
  • The standalone Cleared visual misses what the approach flyout has: no CVA {airport} row at an untowered field, no “field not in sight” greying, no short marks, and runway order (10L first, too short for an A320) rather than the room's (MenuCatalog.cs:1635-1671).
  • The pattern entries have an “(other)” companion too (SharedMenuGroups.cs:683, MenuCatalog.BuildPatternEntryOther :812): already a flyout, so not a ShowListPopup caller, but decision 2 decides it as well.
  • “No STARs” is not rare. Livermore (KLVK) and Half Moon Bay (KHAF) have runways and approaches but no STAR in the bundled CIFP, so Join STAR's free-text fallback is reached by any IFR arrival there.
  • CTO Custom's blank send repeats a row. Both takeoff sets start with Cleared for takeoff CTO (TakeoffFlyout.cs:230, :255), which is what Custom… sends blank (BlankInput.Submits, MenuCatalog.cs:766). Its popup box is 160 px (MenuPopups.cs:61) under a 41-character placeholder (:479).
off: visual rows grey “field not in sight” until Force is on. decision 5; off: a blank Custom box sends nothing.
