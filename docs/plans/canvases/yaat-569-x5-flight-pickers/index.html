Flight picker flyouts: heading, altitude, speed

The strip's busiest buttons, Fly heading, Maintain altitude and Assign speed, still open popups drawn by MenuPopups: a 120 px list of 72 headings, the titled rich list of altitudes with its MVA line, and a 120 px list of speeds. Turn left / right and the degree turns are the same list popup in All Commands, and Speed…, Temporary altitude… and Cruise… are one-box input popups. This page draws each decision as lanes A, B (and C), the recommended lane first. The owner has judged the page: decisions 1b to 6 went to lane A, marked “chosen”; decision 1 stays open, and lane D draws the compass control the owner asked to see.

Every flyout draws the built parts: the Fluent dark menu, the title line and glyph rows of the approach flyouts, the boxes of the takeoff and approach flyouts, the FlyoutTabStrip tabs, the 480 px cap, the flight glyphs of QuickCommandGlyphs. The aircraft are UAL455, an A320 at FL240, 280 kt, heading 270, and N738SP, a C172 at 3,500, 100 kt, assigned 4,000, both bound for KOAK (field elevation 9 ft). Click a cell, a row, a chip or a tab, and type in the boxes, to see what each sends.

  • All Commands. Each All Commands entry opens the same flyout, built by the same builder.

Recommended, not drawn as lanes

  • Keyboard. recommended: focus lands in the flyout's box on open, as Follow's filter box does, since a heading, an altitude or a speed is fastest typed; the ● cell or row is marked but not focused. Down moves from the box into the grid, the list or the chips at the ● mark; in the heading grid the arrows move one cell (5°) left or right and one row (60°) up or down; Up from the top row returns to the box; Enter sends the focused cell or row, or what the box holds; Escape closes the flyout back to its strip button. Where a flyout has tabs (decision 4 lane C), Left and Right switch tabs from the rows, as in X2b.
  • Glyphs. recommended: the flyouts reuse the strip's flight glyphs: Fly heading's compass on heading rows and the heading box, Maintain's climbing step on altitude boxes, Assign speed's dial on speed rows. No new glyph, except the draft in decision 5.
  • Screen edge and width. Already ruled (X2b ruling 5): FlipX | SlideY, no shrinking of lists, the 480 px cap.
  • Command text. The grid and the heading box send three-digit headings (FH 090); today's list sends the bare number (FH 90), and the sim reads both. Altitude rows send bare feet as today (CM 25000); the Temporary altitude and Cruise boxes send hundreds as today (TEMPALT 120).
