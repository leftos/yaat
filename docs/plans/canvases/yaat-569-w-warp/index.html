YAAT-1018 · family W · Warp · mock for the owner's decisions

Warp flyout

Family W is the last popup with its own form: Warp… (the end of All Commands, for any controllable aircraft) and Warp here on the point menu at a map point, which opens the same form seeded with the point's fix-radial-distance. Both send WARP {frd} {heading} {altitude} {speed}. At a taxi node Warp here is already a one-click row sending WARPG #id and stays one.

The page draws the form as a flyout of four box rows under a title line, the command column showing what each field puts in the command and, on the last row, the whole command Enter sends. One artboard per decision, each option a lane; boxes and chips are live.

Commands, the parser's slot rules and the handler's behaviour are read from the source. The aircraft, positions and FRDs are illustrative; the mock knows a handful of Bay Area fixes, and its “Not a known fix” stands for the nav-data check.

Measured from the code (static reads, not run)

  • WARP's heading is magnetic. The parser wraps it as a MagneticHeading (src/Yaat.Sim/Commands/CommandParser.cs:2610); the handler turns it true with heading.ToTrue(aircraft.Declination) for the aircraft's heading, track and target (src/Yaat.Sim/Commands/FlightCommandHandler.cs:1018-1019, :1023) and keeps it as the assigned magnetic heading (:1024). The menu seeds the box with the aircraft's magnetic heading (MenuCatalog.cs:1091-1097), and the FRD's radial is magnetic too (FrdResolver.cs:29, :172-176).
  • The declination it uses is the one cached for where the aircraft was. The handler moves the aircraft (FlightCommandHandler.cs:1017) and converts with aircraft.Declination on the next line; that cache is refreshed only by FlightPhysics.RefreshDeclinationCache once the aircraft has moved more than 0.02° (FlightPhysics.cs:107-138). Within the Bay Area the difference is a fraction of a degree; for a cross-country warp the first tick's true heading is off by the difference between the two declinations. What the heading hold does on the next tick is unmeasured.
  • Heading, altitude and speed are optional in the sim. WARP <fix|FRD> [heading] [altitude] [speed], one to four words (CommandParser.cs:2521-2539); an omitted value is null and the handler keeps the aircraft's present magnetic heading, altitude and indicated airspeed (FlightCommandHandler.cs:1001-1003). COMMANDS.md documents it (:722, :2347-2360). Only the fix is required (“WARP requires a fix”, CommandParser.cs:2528-2531).
  • The trailing values are slot-filled, left to right. A word is a heading only if it is a whole number 1-360 (CommandParser.cs:2574-2583); otherwise it is tried as an altitude through AltitudeResolver (:2585-2595), then as a speed, any positive whole number (:2597-2605); a word no slot takes fails (:2607). So a middle value cannot be left out: WARP SUNOL 250 reads 250 as a heading.
  • An altitude under 1,000 is hundreds. AltitudeResolver.Resolve multiplies any number under 1000 by 100 (AltitudeResolver.cs:19-27). Altitudes from 1 to 999 ft go into a WARP only as whole hundreds, 001 to 009.

Found while drawing

  • Today's popup sends feet the sim reads as hundreds. Its box is labelled “Altitude (feet)” and seeded with the altitude in feet (MenuPopups.cs:262, :327; MenuCatalog.cs:1103). N123AB at 800 ft in the KPAO pattern opens with 800, and OK sends WARP … 800 …: 80,000 ft.
  • Today's popup closes before it checks, then says nothing. Submit closes the popup and returns if the FRD is blank or any number fails int.TryParse (MenuPopups.cs:265-277). A parked aircraft's speed seeds blank (indicated airspeed 0, PositiveOrBlank, :427), so Warp… on SWA2471 at the gate does nothing on OK until a speed is typed. A heading of 0 parses, goes out, and the sim refuses it after the popup has gone (“invalid warp argument '0'”, CommandParser.cs:2607).
  • WARP always leaves the aircraft airborne with its phases, route and procedure cleared (FlightCommandHandler.cs:1005-1016, :1035-1036), and Warp… is offered for any controllable aircraft, on the ground too (MenuCatalog.cs:1070, AircraftCommandApplicability.cs:131). A parked aircraft seeds its field elevation as altitude: KOAK's 9 ft reads as 900 ft.
  • The sim takes any positive speed (CommandParser.cs:2599): 2500 warps a C172 at 2,500 kt.
  • A seeded FRD is rounded. FrdResolver.ToFrd rounds the radial to a degree and the distance to a whole mile (FrdResolver.cs:176, :182), so a point warp lands up to about half a mile from the click.
