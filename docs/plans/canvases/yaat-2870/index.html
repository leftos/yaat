YAAT-2870 · The YAAT tile on the STARS and ERAM bars: open look questions

One YAAT tile at the right end of the STARS and ERAM bars opens the YAAT-only controls in that bar’s own style (ruled in YAAT-2855 mock lane Q2 B). Five questions remain; pick one option per question. Each lane shows the bar over a dark scope in a 1280 px Radar window, drawn at the sizes and colours the code uses. Tile labels for YAAT entries are the classic bar’s labels split into lines; values, map names and callsigns are examples. Pink dashed notes are mock annotations, not UI.

  1. Q1 STARS: where the YAAT controls open: A YAAT page with its own page per menu · B band under the DCB
  2. Q2 ERAM: where the YAAT button sits: A empty cell (1,8) · B tenth full-height column
  3. Q3 Look of a torn-off YAAT control: A classic navy copy · B copy in its bar’s style
  4. Q5 STARS DEL TEAROFF tile: A none (today) · B tile after SHIFT

Q1 · AYAAT page, with a page of its own per menu tile

The YAAT tile swaps the Main row for a YAAT page of DCB tiles: OTHERS, AUTO, TDM and MVA act in place, and PROFILE, TEMPLATE, TOOLS, DISPLAY, DB, VIEWS and PILOT LENS each open a page of their own.
That page dims the YAAT tiles up to its opener, as MAPS dims RANGE to MAPS; DONE or Esc goes back one page, and DONE on the YAAT page returns to Main.

Q2 · AYAAT button in the empty cell (1,8)

YAAT fills the Master Toolbar’s empty last cell in the second row, so the bar keeps its 2×9 size; opening it hides the other buttons in place and its two-row menu rises into the bar, as CRC does for a second-row menu.
A menu entry (DB here) opens its submenu to its right in the coral outline; YAAT again closes the menu.

YAAT menu entries. Dropped as duplicates of ERAM VIEWS / DB FIELDS: VIEWS›LA/CA/MCI (ERAM VIEWS›CONFLCT ALERT presses the same view), VIEWS›CRR (ERAM VIEWS›CRR), DB›4TH LINE (ERAM DB FIELDS›DEST and TYPE press it). Also dropped, held outside those two menus: DB›CHAR (ERAM FONT›FDB steps it), DISPLAY›WX (ERAM ATC TOOLS›WX).
