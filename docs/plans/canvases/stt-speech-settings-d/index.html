YAAT-1602 part D: Settings > Speech, the eight open on-screen decisions

Each section below is one decision. Its lanes (A, B and sometimes C) draw only the part of the Speech section or the status bar that the decision changes, at real size and in the look of the approved lane C mock (docs/plans/canvases/stt-speech-settings/index.html). Lane A is the recommendation, and the reason is the green line under the lanes. A dashed yellow outline marks the element that differs between lanes.

Sizes come from the shipped manifest (src/Yaat.Client/Speech/stt-downloads.json): fp32 is 2,550,014,451 bytes in 8 files, which is 2.55 GB, or 2,432 MB in the installer's 1024 × 1024 units. int8 is 0.67 GB. The installer's progress text at 42% is therefore "Downloading 42% (1,021 / 2,432 MB)". The GPU figures are CUDA 13's minimums: driver 580 or newer, and compute capability 7.5 or newer (Turing).

Already ruled, so not offered here:

  • "View licence" opens the model's LICENSE file once downloaded, and the upstream model page before that.
  • "Built with Llama" sits on the card of the model that uses it.
  • Downloads keep running when Settings closes.
  • Detecting a GPU fallback at engine load is out of scope.
