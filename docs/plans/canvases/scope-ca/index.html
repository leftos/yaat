Static mock of the instructor's Scope. Blocks follow ScopeTemplates.Stars / ScopeTemplates.Eram and the ScopeRenderer look: black background, bold 13 px monospace text in the block colour, blue track circle, leader to the text bounds, white vector lines.

How to read each lane. Three frames of the same moment: Live flashes at the Scope's blink rate (MapBlink.IsOn: 0.5 s on, 0.5 s off), then still frames of the on and off phases, so a screenshot shows both.
  • Colours: white for the viewer's tracks, green (50,205,50) for others' and unowned tracks, red (255,60,60) for the CA line and the conflict ring (ScopeRenderer, TargetRenderer).
  • STARS CA line: CA h.h/vvv (DatablockText.BuildConflictLine); the Scope draws it as the block's last line (CRC STARS draws it above the callsign). It flashes by blanking on the off phase.
  • ERAM STCA draws no CA line: the whole block flashes (every text blank on the off phase), eram.md:375. ERAM has no acknowledged state.
  • The scale bar in each frame gives the miles. Q3 and Q4 draw no ring on ERAM pairs, so they can be judged apart from Q1.
