YAAT Scope: where the map tools and the weather text go (YAAT-2465)

Two questions for the owner. A: where the new map tools sit on the toolbar (MVA toggle, pan/zoom LOCK, OFF CNTR, FIX overlay, WX weather toggle, DCNF deconfliction, and the range ring RR with SHOW, PLACE RR, size 2–20 and RR CNTR). B: where the weather (METAR) text sits on the scope, now that the SSA card holds the top-left corner the Radar used.

Each lane is the default 1460 px Scope window under the STARS profile (the SSA floating at the top-left, LA/CA/MCI in the 360 px dock), drawn at 1:1 in the toolbar's own font, tiles and colours (ScopeToolbarStyles.axaml, the half-tile pairing rule of ScopeToolbar.HalfTiles). Bar widths are measured from this page's render.

Question A: where the new map tools go

A1RECOMMENDEDMVA tile + DISPLAY menu + RR tile, inserted after TDM

Three new tiles after TDM. MVA and DISPLAY ▸ pair up as one half-tile column; RR ▸ shows its size, so it is a full tile (RR OFF when hidden). DB/VIEWS, BRITE, PREF and DEL TEAROFF keep their shapes. Shown with DISPLAY open: its tray (FIX, WX, LOCK, DCNF, OFF CNTR) hangs from the window's left edge over the top of the scope, like every tray, so it covers the SSA card while open. The inset shows the RR tray, which takes the same place when RR is pressed instead.

A2One bar tile per feature, appended after the existing tiles

Seven new tiles (MVA, LOCK, OFF CNTR, FIX, WX, DCNF, RR) named after DEL TEAROFF in the profile; PILOT LENS, which the STARS profile does not name, stays last. The pairing rule now pairs DEL TEAROFF with MVA as half tiles, and DCNF is left a lone full tile. At the default 1460 px window the bar still fits (measured below), so nothing scrolls there; the strips under the window show the same bar in a window narrowed to 700 px, where the chevrons appear: at its left end, and scrolled to its right end.

A3Everything inside the existing TOOLS tray, beside the Rings tool

The bar is unchanged. TOOLS' tray holds the Rings tool (J-RING, CONE, DRI 5, DRI 3, SIZE) and then the ten new controls. Opening TOOLS arms the band's ring tool at once (J-RING lit, ScopeViewModel.OnOpenMenuChanged), so reaching for WX or LOCK leaves a click on a track placing a J-ring until the tray closes.

Question B: where the weather (METAR) text sits

B1RECOMMENDEDBottom-left, green mono 14 px, lines growing upward

Both B lanes show the bar as A1 would leave it. The Radar's lines (Consolas 14, rgb(0,200,0), 18 px apart, 10 px from the left), anchored to the bottom so a new station adds a line above. The SSA card and the STARS entry area keep the top-left. Under the ERAM and MIXED profiles the Response Area and MCA sit in this corner (dashed outline, drawn from ScopeEntryLayer.axaml), so the text would need to sit above them there.

B2Top-left, directly under the SSA card

The same lines placed under the SSA card at the scope's top-left. The STARS entry and response area also sits under the SSA's default place (16, 86, JetBrains Mono 19, ScopeEntryLayer.axaml), shown here with a sample response: the two share the corner whenever an entry is typed.
