## Context

`docs/plans/MAIN.md` → Current focus → **shrink `IActionHost`** ([`04-relocation.md`](X:/dev/yaat/docs/plans/tick-path/04-relocation.md) line 19). TDLS (commit A) and strips (commit B) crossed 2026-09-07; the remaining slots are coordination, ASDE-X/SAID, CRR groups, bookmarks, the clock, and `IHostSteps` still carries `LiveTrafficSync`, `CoordinationTimers`, `TowerLists`. Coordination is next in the documented order, and `TowerLists` shares its CRC topic.

**Why coordination is smaller than strips/TDLS:** its *state* has already crossed. `CoordinationChannel`/`CoordinationItem`/`StarsCoordinationStatus` are Sim types, `SimScenarioState.CoordinationChannels` is snapshotted (`ScenarioSnapshotDto.CoordinationChannels` via `CoordinationChannelSnapshotMapper`), timing is in `ElapsedSeconds` (no wall clock, no `Attendance`). Only three things are still server-side: the verb bodies (`yaat-server/src/Yaat.Server/Simulation/CoordinationCommandHandler.cs`), the tick step (`TickProcessor.ProcessCoordinationTimers`, `TickProcessor.cs:205-253`), and the broadcast glue (`RoomHost.cs:103-111, 203-210, 277-285`). `TowerListTracker` is likewise already `SimulationEngine.TowerListTracker`; only its call site (`TickProcessor.ProcessTowerLists`, `:255-270`) and its unsnapshotted dwell entries remain.

**Defects fixed on the way (found by the exploration):**
1. `CoordinationItem.Id` is `Guid.NewGuid()` at `RD`/`RDH` (`CoordinationCommandHandler.cs:116,179`) and not baked — live and a reconstruction hold the same item under different ids (the `SEP`/`HSC`/`SCAN` class of bug). No oracle fixture scripts a coordination verb, which is why it is invisible.
2. `TowerListTracker._entries` (per-callsign `EnteredAtSeconds`) is not in any snapshot, so a restore mid-stream re-enters every aircraft at the restore second and the P-list sort order changes.
3. STARS click-to-track removes coordination twice: `CrcClientState.Stars.cs:262` calls `RoomEngine.RemoveCoordinationOnTrack` after `RecordAndDispatch("TRACK")`, whose Track arm already fires `OnTrackAcquired` → `RemoveOnRadarAcquisition` + broadcast.
4. Dead code: `CoordinationCommandHandler.IsCoordinationCommand`/`IsGlobalCoordinationCommand` (nothing calls them; the classifier uses `TrackEngine.IsCoordinationCommand`), `AttendanceActionHost.AcquiredCallsigns` (never asserted).
5. Stale docs: `TickOracleTests.cs:339` says coordination is "outside the snapshot" (only ASDE-X is); yaat-server `CLAUDE.md` says "5min after ack, 2min warning" (code: 180 s / warning at 120 s remaining / recall lingers 10 s).

## Decisions (user, 2026-09-07)

- **Scope:** two sub-commits — **C** coordination bodies, **D** tower lists (state into the snapshot + Sim step). One broadcast seam designed once.
- **Item id:** deterministic `"{ListId}-{SequenceNumber}"` — `NextSequence` is already snapshotted, so no draw exists to bake and the oracle fixture can see coordination byte-identical. No new `BakedDraws` field.
- **Seam:** a dirty flag, not an item-level change set. `IStateChangeConsumer.OnCoordinationChanged()` (payload-less); `RoomHost` re-pushes the whole `StarsCoordination` topic, which is what the wire carries anyway (`CrcBroadcastService.BuildCoordinationData` sends every channel plus every tower list in one payload).
- **Timing constants named, values unchanged:** `SimScenarioState.CoordinationAckExpirySeconds = 180`, `CoordinationExpiryWarningSeconds = 120`, `CoordinationRecallLingerSeconds = 10` (precedent: `PointoutNoActionSeconds`). The README's "warning fires at two-thirds of the ack's life" finding stays its own item with its own aviation review — this slice is baseline-neutral in behaviour.
