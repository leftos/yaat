### Between E4 and E5 — trainer question, t≈147

- Trainer: "is there a general bug where aircraft that call for push never call for taxi after that, even after a delay?" then "They do need to spool up their engines, to e fair, and I think we talked about some kind of delay in the past".

### E5 — t=188, trigger pilot_call, SWA5456

- Picture: SWA5456 holding on TE nose south; "pushed back from gate 29, with information Alpha, IFR to Denver Airport, ready to taxi." ASA847 pushing on S; SWA919 holding on TC; NKS8659 ~8 nm final 30, N33027 final 28R.
- Candidates: SWA5456 only.
- Proposal: `SWA5456 TAXI TE U W W1 30` — "runway three zero, taxi via TE, U, W, W1" (7110.65 3-7-2); pathfinder resolves it, one hold-short (30 at W1). Watch W for NKS8659's rollout and exit.
- Verdict: corrected to `SWA5456 TAXI U W 30`. Trainer: "You wouldn't issue TE because it's non-movement area so you can't issue it. ... it's not important to you how they join the movement area on U, just that they do, so you would issue your taxi starting at U. You also wouldn't issue W1. Per the 7110, a taxiway connecting to the full length intersection of a runway can be ommitted if it's a numbered variation of the taxiway before that. So "runway 30 via U W", but "runway 28R via C B" (because B, the full length intersection of 28R, is not a numbered variation of C)."
- Result: sent, success; response "Taxi via TE U W RWY 30" — YAAT put TE back into the route it reports.
- Lessons: (1) a taxi route starts at the first movement-area taxiway; non-movement taxilanes are not issued (the last one may be, to pick the entry point, rarely worth it). (2) The full-length connector is left out when it is a numbered variation of the taxiway before it (W → W1: "via U, W"); kept when it is not ("28R via C, B").

### E10 — t=302, initiative, SWA5456
