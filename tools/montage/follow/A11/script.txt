# A11 — FOLLOW with no traffic in sight is refused; FOLLOWF is accepted.
# KOAK runway 28R, right traffic. Two VFR C172s inbound from the northwest in trail,
# about 1.2 NM apart, neither placed on a leg by the scenario.
#
#   N52417  lead     — joins the right downwind first and turns base
#   N738SP  follower — joins the same downwind behind it; never given a traffic advisory
#
# Times are sim seconds. See card.md for what the clip shows.

# t=1  Inhibit conflict alerts on both aircraft (CAINH toggles).
t=1 N52417 CAINH
t=1 N738SP CAINH

# t=2  Both: enter the right downwind for 28R, lead first.
t=2 N52417 ERD 28R
t=3 N738SP ERD 28R

# t=102 Lead: cleared to land (first in the sequence).
t=102 N52417 CLAND 28R

# t=195 The follow, with the lead on base and the follower on the downwind. No traffic
#       advisory was issued, so the follower has not reported the traffic in sight: refused.
t=195 N738SP FOLLOW N52417

# t=200 The forced follow: FOLLOWF skips the traffic-in-sight check (the instructor vouches
#       for it) and is accepted.
t=200 N738SP FOLLOWF N52417

# t=204 Follower: cleared to land; it applies when the follower joins the final behind.
t=204 N738SP CLAND 28R
