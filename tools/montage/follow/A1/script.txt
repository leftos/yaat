# A1 — right-downwind follower behind a straight-in on a 2 NM final.
# KOAK runway 28R, right traffic. Two VFR C172s, both inbound from the northwest,
# neither placed on a leg by the scenario. The commands below fly them in.
#
#   N52417  lead     — C172 on the 28R extended centerline, straight in
#   N738SP  follower — C172 northwest of the field, joins the right downwind
#
# Times are sim seconds. See card.md for what the clip shows.

# t=2  Lead: join the 28R final straight-in, ~5.5 NM out.
t=2 N52417 EF 28R

# t=3  Follower: enter the right downwind for 28R, 2.5 NM back of the abeam.
t=3 N738SP ERD 28R

# t=4  Lead: cleared to land (it is on final, first in the sequence).
t=4 N52417 CLAND 28R

# t=100 Traffic advisory to the follower: C172 on final, 3 miles. Required before
#        FOLLOW can be accepted — a pilot cannot follow traffic not in sight.
t=100 N738SP RTIS FINAL 3 28R C172

# t=142 The follow itself, with the lead at ~2 NM final and the follower on the downwind.
t=142 N738SP FOLLOW N52417

# t=144 Follower: cleared to land. Armed against the follow — it is applied when the
#        follower joins 28R's final behind the traffic.
t=144 N738SP CLAND 28R
