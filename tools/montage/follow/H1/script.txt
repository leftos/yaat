# H1 — ground follow through a turn and a runway hold short.
# KOAK north field, departures from runway 28R. Two VFR C172s parked at the GA T-hangars west of runway 33,
# neither placed on a taxiway by the scenario. The commands below taxi them out.
#
#   N52417  lead     — parked at GA13, taxis F, C, crossing runway 33 at C, then B to runway 28R
#   N738SP  follower — parked at GA16, told to follow the lead with no route of its own
#
# Times are sim seconds. See card.md for what the clip shows.

# t=1  Inhibit conflict alerts on both aircraft (CAINH toggles), as every montage clip does.
t=1 N52417 CAINH
t=1 N738SP CAINH

# t=2  Lead: taxi to runway 28R via F, C, B, cleared across runway 33 at C.
t=2 N52417 TAXI F C B CROSS 33 RWY 28R

# t=20 Follower: follow the Cessna. It starts up and falls in behind the lead on F; the follow carries no
#      runway crossing clearance, so it stops at the runway 33 holding position on C the lead was cleared across.
t=20 N738SP FOLLOWG N52417

# t=165 Follower: cleared across runway 33, once the lead is clear of it. It crosses and resumes the follow.
t=165 N738SP CROSS 33
