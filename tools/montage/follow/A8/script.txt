# A8 — 28L left-downwind follower told to follow a 28R straight-in.
# KOAK close parallels: 28L flies left traffic (south side), 28R right traffic (north side).
# Two VFR C172s, neither placed on a leg by the scenario. The commands below fly them in.
#
#   N52417  lead     — C172 east of the field on the 28R extended centerline, straight in
#   N738SP  follower — C172 west of the field, inbound from San Carlos along the shoreline, joins 28L's left downwind
#
# Times are sim seconds. See card.md for what the clip shows.

# t=1  Inhibit conflict alerts on both aircraft (CAINH toggles).
t=1 N52417 CAINH
t=1 N738SP CAINH

# t=2  Lead: join the 28R final straight-in.
t=2 N52417 EF 28R

# t=3  Follower: enter the left downwind for 28L.
t=3 N738SP ELD 28L

# t=4  Lead: cleared to land 28R (first in the sequence).
t=4 N52417 CLAND 28R

# t=100 Traffic advisory to the follower: the Cessna at 11 o'clock, 4 miles, westbound on the 28R straight-in.
t=100 N738SP RTIS 11 4 W C172

# t=142 The follow, once the traffic is called in sight: the follower on the 28L downwind, the lead on a 2 NM final for 28R.
t=142 N738SP FOLLOW N52417

# t=144 Follower: cleared to land 28R, the lead's runway, behind the traffic.
t=144 N738SP CLAND 28R
