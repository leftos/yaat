# C1 — right-base follower told to follow a free-flight lead whose own right base is still queued.
# KOAK runway 28R, right traffic. Two VFR C172s, neither placed on a leg by the scenario.
#
#   N52417  lead     — C172 11 NM east of the field, inbound direct Lake Chabot (VPCBT), then a right base for 28R
#   N738SP  follower — C172 north-east of Lake Chabot, joins a wide right base for 28R from present position
#
# At the FOLLOW the lead has no runway yet: it is a free-flight lead with its pattern entry queued behind DCT VPCBT.
# Times are sim seconds. See card.md for what the clip shows.

# t=1  Inhibit conflict alerts on both aircraft (CAINH toggles).
t=1 N52417 CAINH
t=1 N738SP CAINH

# t=2  Lead: direct Lake Chabot, then enter a right base for 28R from there.
t=2 N52417 DCT VPCBT; ERB 28R

# t=3  Follower: enter a right base for 28R from present position.
t=3 N738SP ERB 28R

# t=30 Traffic advisories, both ways: the Cessna at 11 o'clock, 3 miles, westbound for the follower; the Cessna at
#      2 o'clock, 3 miles, southbound for the lead. Both call the traffic in sight inside 3 miles.
t=30 N738SP RTIS 11 3 W C172
t=30 N52417 RTIS 2 3 S C172

# t=90 The follow, with the lead about 1.6 NM south of the follower and still bound for Lake Chabot.
t=90 N738SP FOLLOW N52417

# t=215 Lead: cleared to land 28R once its queued right base has begun (first in the sequence).
t=215 N52417 CLAND 28R

# t=260 Follower: cleared to land 28R, behind the traffic.
t=260 N738SP CLAND 28R
