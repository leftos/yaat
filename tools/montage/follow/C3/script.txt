# C3 — C172 pursuing a B738 extends past the jet's base turn.
# KOAK runway 30 (10,507 ft; the airliner runway), right traffic. A VFR B738 and a VFR C172, neither placed on a leg
# by the scenario.
#
#   SWA2471 lead     — B738 at 2,500 ft and 200 kt, 9 NM east of Lake Chabot (VPCBT), inbound direct Lake Chabot
#   N738SP  follower — C172 at 1,500 ft and 100 kt on the same inbound, 3 NM ahead of the jet, which overtakes it
#
# The jet passes over the Cessna, the Cessna is told to follow it, and the jet is turned onto a right base for 30 at
# once, less than half a mile ahead of the Cessna: the Cessna has not got the jet's spacing when the jet turns.
# Times are sim seconds. See card.md for what the clip shows.

# t=1  Inhibit conflict alerts on both aircraft (CAINH toggles).
t=1 SWA2471 CAINH
t=1 N738SP CAINH

# t=1  Traffic advisories, both ways: the Cessna at 12 o'clock, 3 miles, westbound for the jet; the 737 at 6 o'clock,
#      3 miles, westbound for the Cessna, which calls it in sight once the jet has passed.
t=1 SWA2471 RTIS 12 3 W C172
t=1 N738SP RTIS 6 3 W B738

# t=2  Both aircraft: direct Lake Chabot.
t=2 SWA2471 DCT VPCBT
t=3 N738SP DCT VPCBT

# t=112 The follow, with the jet just past the Cessna, 0.3 NM ahead.
t=112 N738SP FOLLOW SWA2471

# t=114 Jet: enter a right base for runway 30 from present position.
t=114 SWA2471 ERB 30

# t=140 Jet: cleared to land 30 (first in the sequence).
t=140 SWA2471 CLAND 30

# t=230 Follower: cleared to land 30, behind the jet.
t=230 N738SP CLAND 30
